About

Building at the intersection of hardware and autonomy.

Hi, I’m Jake. I’m a Mechanical Engineering student at The University of Texas at Austin with a minor in Entrepreneurship.

I’m interested in building hardware for autonomous systems, robotics, and aerospace, especially when mechanical design has to work closely with sensing, software, and electronics. At UT, I conduct experimental propulsion research in the Underwood Laboratory and help lead mechanical design for Texas Marine Robotics.

Outside of engineering, I’m drawn to startups and entrepreneurship. After spending a summer at an early-stage AI startup in New York, I realized how much I enjoy small teams where people have real ownership and move quickly. Long term, I hope to bring that approach to building ambitious physical technology.

I grew up in Westfield, New Jersey, and now call Austin home. Outside of work, I enjoy playing golf and tennis and staying involved in service and philanthropy.
